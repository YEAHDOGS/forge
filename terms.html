What Forge is

Forge is a free, open-source wizard that helps you prepare an Oklahoma LLC filing: a printable Articles of Organization worksheet, filing instructions, a fee total, and a post-filing checklist.

What Forge is not

  • Not legal advice. Forge is not a law firm, and nothing it produces is legal advice or a substitute for an attorney.
  • Files nothing. Forge submits nothing to any state. You are responsible for your own filing at sos.ok.gov.

Fees and accuracy

Fee data was verified against sos.ok.gov on 2026-09-09. Fees change; verify every figure before you pay. Forge is provided as-is, without warranties of any kind.

Your data

The wizard stores your answers in your browser on your device only. This site collects nothing (see the privacy policy).

Acceptable use

Use Forge to prepare your own filings. Do not misrepresent Forge output as legal advice or as a filing made on anyone's behalf.
