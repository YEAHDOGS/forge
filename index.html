Texas warning

An Oklahoma LLC transacting business in Texas must register as a foreign LLC: $750 plus a 2.7% card fee, with late fees after 90 days. Talk to a Texas business attorney before choosing Oklahoma if you will operate in Texas.

FAQ

Asked, answered.

Is Forge really free?

Yes. The wizard is free and open source. No account, no paywall, no upsell. You pay only the state fees when you file, directly to the state.

Does Forge file my LLC for me?

No. Forge prepares everything: a filled worksheet, exact filing instructions, and a live fee total. You take the worksheet and file at sos.ok.gov yourself. Online filing takes about one business day.

Is this legal advice?

No. Forge is not a law firm and nothing it produces is legal advice. If your situation is complicated, talk to a business attorney.

Why Oklahoma only?

Every fee and rule in Forge is verified against the Oklahoma Secretary of State. More states are added only after their fee data is verified, never before. Unverified states are marked coming soon with no numbers at all.

What if I operate in Texas?

An Oklahoma LLC transacting business in Texas must register as a foreign LLC: $750 plus a 2.7% card fee, with late fees after 90 days. Forge flags this in the wizard and advises talking to a Texas business attorney before choosing Oklahoma.

Where does my information go?

Nowhere. The wizard runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are saved on your device only, and nothing is sent to any server.
